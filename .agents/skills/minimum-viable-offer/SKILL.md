# Minimum Viable Offer

Use this to design the paid offer before designing the product.

## Steps

1. Define the buyer and painful job.
2. Define the paid outcome.
3. Decide what is delivered manually.
4. Remove non-essential features.
5. Price the offer as a validation test.
6. Define success and kill criteria.

## Output

- Buyer.
- Painful job.
- Offer.
- Deliverables.
- Price rationale.
- What to exclude.
- Success criteria.
- Kill criteria.
