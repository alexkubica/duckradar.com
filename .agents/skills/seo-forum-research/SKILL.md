# SEO Forum Research

Use this for cautious market research outside Reddit. Prefer public pages, search-result snippets, official directory listings, RSS feeds, saved pages, or user-supplied exports. Treat forums as community evidence, not proof of willingness to pay.

## Guardrails

- Check robots and terms posture before automated collection from any forum.
- Do not use login cookies, paid/private areas, CAPTCHA bypass, proxy rotation, fingerprint spoofing, or account automation.
- Do not scrape member profiles for direct outreach.
- Store minimal public evidence: URL, title, forum/source, date if visible, short excerpt/summary, public handle only when needed for attribution.
- Lead lists must use public business identity, website, and non-forum contact path. Forum usernames alone are not leads.
- Separate competitor/tool evidence from buyer pain evidence.

## Workflow

1. Define the research question: offer validation, competitor copy, workflow pain, pricing, ICP narrowing, or lead discovery.
2. Search public web and site-specific pages first.
3. For each promising thread/listing/article, capture:
   - URL and source type
   - direct pain or tool category
   - buyer language
   - mentioned tools/competitors
   - workflow or pricing implication
   - lead-source clue needing non-forum verification
4. Prefer current sources, but keep older forum posts only when the workflow pain is durable.
5. Synthesize into:
   - repeated patterns
   - copyable product patterns
   - offer implication
   - competitor/tool list
   - validation leads and outreach angle
   - confidence and sample limitations

## Output

- Evidence summary with source links.
- Tools to copy or benchmark.
- Buyer/workflow pain.
- Offer implication.
- Lead list with public business/site evidence outside the forum.
- Confidence score and data gaps.
