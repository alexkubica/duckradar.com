# Sales Discovery

Use this to validate whether prospects have current pain and budget.

## Call Flow

1. Ask what SEO tools they pay for today.
2. Ask which features they actually use weekly.
3. Ask what feels overpriced, underused, or hard to justify.
4. Ask what client questions are hardest to answer.
5. Ask whether AI visibility comes up in sales or reporting.
6. Ask what they tried already.
7. Ask what would make this worth buying this month.
8. Offer the monthly subscription only if pain is specific and current.

## Output

- Qualification result.
- Pain summary.
- Budget/willingness-to-pay signal.
- Objections.
- Next step.
