# SEO Claim Guardrail

Use this on every marketing page, report, sales script, and product claim.

## Rules

- Do not promise rankings.
- Do not promise ChatGPT visibility.
- Do not promise deterministic AI Overview or AI Mode inclusion.
- Do not call llms.txt, special schema, chunking, or fake mentions required.
- Prefer "monitor," "sample," "evidence," "directional," and "prioritize."
- Cite Google guidance when discussing AI Overviews or AI Mode.

## Output

- Claim risk assessment.
- Safer rewrite.
- Required source links.
- Remaining caveats.
