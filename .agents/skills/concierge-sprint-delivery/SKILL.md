# Concierge Subscription Delivery

Use this for manual monthly Search Visibility Pulse delivery before automation exists.

## Inputs

- GSC read-only access.
- GA4 read-only access when available.
- Domain.
- 2 competitors.
- 3 money pages.
- 10 high-intent keywords or prompts.
- ICP and customer context.

## Steps

1. Identify the top 5 GSC opportunities: rank 5-20, rising impressions, low CTR, decaying pages.
2. Compare competitor pages and SERP formats.
3. Sample AI/search visibility across 10 agreed prompts or keywords.
4. Identify technical or indexing red flags.
5. Produce 3 prioritized actions.
6. Produce 1 content or refresh brief.
7. Ask whether adding another subscribed site would be valuable.

## Output

- 1-page client-ready action brief.
- Prioritized action list.
- Content/update brief.
- Retention and multi-site expansion notes.
