# Mom Test Research

## Core Rule

Ask about real past behavior, current workflow, recent examples, constraints, and money already spent.

Do not ask people to judge the idea, predict future usage, or reassure you.

## Use For

Use this before sending DuckRadar research outreach, reviewing interview notes, or turning prospect replies into product evidence.

## Good Questions

Ask:

1. How do you currently do this?
2. When did this last happen?
3. What did you do last time?
4. What tools, templates, or workarounds do you use?
5. What is annoying, slow, risky, or embarrassing about it?
6. What happens if you skip it?
7. Who sees the output?
8. What have you already paid for or tried?
9. What would make this easier next month?

## Bad Questions

Avoid:

1. Would you use this?
2. Would this be useful?
3. Would you pay for this?
4. Do you like this idea?
5. Does this sound interesting?
6. What features would you want?
7. Would AI visibility reporting help your clients?

These invite compliments, guesses, and politeness.

## First Touch Shape

Use this structure:

```text
One concrete observation from their site.

I am researching how people like you handle [workflow].

One question about current behavior.
```

Only mention DuckRadar as context. Do not pitch price, package, site link, or feature list in the first research message.

## Evidence Scoring

High signal:

1. They describe a recent real workflow.
2. They name tools or templates.
3. They mention time, cost, client friction, missed work, or repeated annoyance.
4. They show something they already use.
5. They ask to see the product after describing the problem.

Low signal:

1. They say the idea sounds useful.
2. They say they might use it.
3. They suggest features without describing a current problem.
4. They compliment the product.
5. They ask for a free audit before explaining their workflow.

## Reply Handling

For every reply, extract:

1. Current workflow.
2. Tools used.
3. Frequency.
4. Pain.
5. Cost or time.
6. Existing workaround.
7. Buyer fit.
8. Next best question.
9. Whether to pitch DuckRadar now, later, or not at all.

## Pitch Timing

Pitch only after the prospect gives workflow evidence.

If they describe pain, say:

```text
That helps. I am building DuckRadar around exactly this workflow: turning Search Console data into a monthly client update draft. Want me to send the current version so you can tell me where it misses?
```

If they do not describe pain, ask one more workflow question or close politely.
