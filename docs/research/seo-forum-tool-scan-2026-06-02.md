# SEO Forum, Tool, and Lead Scan

Date: 2026-06-02

## Research Question

What can DuckRadar copy from current SEO/AEO/GEO tools, forums, blogs, and directories without becoming another bloated SEO suite?

## Source Mix

- Local cached Reddit signal database: 567 rows total, including 217 saved-HTML enriched rows with visible comments.
- Public forums and communities: Local Search Forum, BlackHatWorld, Indie Hackers.
- Blogs/articles: PPC Hero, Google Search Central documentation.
- Directories and marketplaces: AppSumo, TrustMRR, Launch/TryLaunch.
- Public competitor/product pages.

## Evidence Summary

### 1. AI Visibility Reporting Is Real, but Crowded

The market is already filling with AI visibility trackers. Repeated primitives:

- prompt tracking across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Google AI Overviews/AI Mode
- brand mention rate
- competitor share of voice
- cited sources / cited URLs
- sentiment and hallucination checks
- prompt-level screenshots or archived responses
- exports, reports, or white-label dashboards

Examples:

- GeoLens: AI answer visibility, citations, share of voice, exports, agency use case.
  Source: https://geolens.info/
- Linksii: buyer-prompt tracking rather than keyword tracking.
  Source: https://www.linksii.com/
- Genwolf: daily prompt capture with mentions, citations, sentiment, and sources.
  Source: https://www.genwolf.ai/
- Livesov: share of voice, proof links, sentiment, hallucination detection, agency and ecommerce positioning.
  Source: https://livesov.com/
- ModelMention: free AI visibility audit, share of voice, prompt coverage, local/ecommerce/service-business positioning.
  Source: https://modelmention.io/
- Mettle: six-prompt starter scan, top sources, citation heatmaps, weekly signal diff.
  Source: https://mettlegeo.com/ai-search-visibility-tool
- RankScope: agency pricing at $399/mo for 25 client workspaces, 600 prompts, white-label dashboards, client report links.
  Source: https://rankscope.ai/pricing
- SEObolt: $29/mo entry point, GSC analytics plus LLM ranking dashboard.
  Source: https://seobolt.io/

Implication:

DuckRadar should not become a generic AI visibility dashboard. That market is already crowded and price-compressing.

### 3. Forum Evidence Favors Caution and Manual Judgment

Local Search Forum shows local SEOs using LLMs for rewriting, plain-English summaries, and audit prompts, but experienced replies warn that AI audit output still requires manual review and corroboration.

Sources:

- https://localsearchforum.com/threads/ai-usage-for-local-seo.63169/
- https://localsearchforum.com/threads/using-chatgpt-for-google-business-profiles-and-citation-building.62868/

BlackHatWorld shows two useful patterns:

- Practitioners describe GEO tooling as messy and duct-taped: ChatGPT/Jasper, Surfer/Clearscope, GSC scripts, manual edits.
- Marketplace services are already selling free/paid GEO audits, and at least one public user pushed back that a free AI visibility scan did not provide unique/actionable information.

Sources:

- https://www.blackhatworld.com/seo/what-tools-are-you-using-for-geo-right-now.1755117/

Indie Hackers case-style posts echo a solo-consultant need: tracking LLM visibility manually is painful, but it is not a full SEO-stack replacement.

Source:

- https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-i-used-ai-seo-radar-to-track-my-clients-llm-visibility-and-what-actually-happened-GsdkhIYaqGxYrWOQr2ZD

Implication:

The best claim language is "monthly directional visibility layer + action brief," not "rank in ChatGPT."

### 4. Google Guardrail

Google's AI features documentation says existing SEO fundamentals remain relevant for AI Overviews and AI Mode, there are no special extra requirements, and Search Console includes AI feature clicks in the standard Web performance report.

Source:

- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features

Implication:

DuckRadar should keep AI visibility as a sampled diagnostic layer attached to standard SEO work. Avoid "GEO hacks," AI-only schema claims, or deterministic inclusion claims.

## Copyable Product Patterns

Copy these patterns:

- Free or low-friction scan: "See what AI says about your brand/domain."
- Prompt universe setup: category, buyer intent, service, city, competitor.
- Prompt result table: brand mentioned, competitor mentioned, cited sources, answer accuracy, sentiment.
- Source gap map: directories, reviews, listicles, Reddit/forums, GBP, competitor pages.
- GSC tie-in: traffic/conversion trend, branded search, money-page impressions, CTR, decay.
- Monthly client-ready deliverable: not just a dashboard; a short brief with next actions.
- White-label/export path for agencies: PDF, Google Sheet, client link, or copy-ready report section.
- Proof trail: prompt, engine, date, response summary, cited URLs.

Do not copy these yet:

- daily scans
- 600+ prompt tracking
- autonomous agents
- content autopublishing
- backlink/PR execution
- full white-label client portals
- ranking or AI inclusion guarantees

## Recommended Offer Rethink

Keep the price, narrow the buyer, sharpen the job.

### Buyer

Local SEO consultants and micro-agencies managing 3-20 local/service-business client sites.

Secondary segments:

- Shopify/ecommerce SEO consultants
- B2B SaaS SEO consultants

Reason for local-first:

- Query + city + service prompts are concrete.
- Clients understand "who should I call in [city]?"
- GBP, reviews, citations, service pages, and local directories create actionable source gaps.
- Local agencies already sell monthly reporting but clients struggle to interpret it.

### Offer

DuckRadar Local Visibility Brief, `$49/site/mo`.

One monthly client-ready brief for one client site:

- Top GSC opportunities.
- 10 fixed local/service buyer prompts checked across a small AI/search sample.
- Brand vs competitor mention snapshot.
- Cited/source gap map.
- 3 next actions for the agency to execute.
- One content refresh or service-page brief.

### Positioning

> A monthly SEO + AI visibility action brief for local SEO consultants who do not need another enterprise dashboard.

### Exclusions

- no client calls by default
- no ranking guarantees
- no "rank in ChatGPT" claims
- no daily monitoring
- no automated implementation
- no backlink/content execution

### Validation Ask

Use this as the next outreach question:

### Additional Patterns

1. Clients still ask for Google rankings first.

The useful wedge is not "clients are demanding GEO." The better wedge is showing AI visibility as an added discovery/trust layer beside rankings. The strongest buyer language is about showing clients how competitors appear in AI answers, not trying to explain abstract AEO/GEO terminology.

2. AI-search tracking must measure answer quality, not just mentions.

The ecommerce citation thread reinforced a measurement gap: a brand mention without price, product variant, rating, availability, or use-case context can be a weak or even misleading win. This suggests DuckRadar should track "mention quality" in brief form:

- mentioned or missing
- described correctly or incorrectly
- cited source
- competitor also mentioned
- next content/source gap to fix

3. SEO automation demand is strongest around repetitive analysis.

The automation and TechSEO threads repeatedly mention:

- GSC opportunities: high impressions, position 4-15, low CTR
- content decay across 30/60/90 days
- technical change alerts
- duplicate titles/metas/H1s
- broken links, redirects, canonicals, noindex issues
- internal linking gaps
- reporting tasks

This supports the original "monthly decision brief" product more than a standalone AI-search tracker.

4. Agency pain is operational overload.

The agency-production-line thread points to context switching, capacity pressure, templated sheets, and repetitive client work. The offer should speak to reducing monthly reporting/analysis load, not to replacing SEO judgment.

### Updated Interpretation

The strongest offer language is:

> One monthly client-ready local SEO brief from GSC, competitor gaps, and a small AI/source visibility check.

Avoid leading with:

> GEO dashboard

or:

> ChatGPT ranking tracker

The product should feel like a report-production shortcut and prioritization layer, with AI visibility as the differentiating section.
