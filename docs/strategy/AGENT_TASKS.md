## Manager

Owner: `startup_manager`

Deliverables:

- Keep the project focused on validating `$49/site/mo` recurring demand.
- Block product build until paid subscription evidence exists.
- Gate changes against `docs/strategy/REQUIREMENTS.md` and `docs/strategy/VALIDATION_PLAN.md`.

## Role Tasks

### Role: `product_strategist`

Objective:

Turn the subscription offer into a tight productized workflow.

Required deliverables:

- Final offer spec.
- Onboarding requirements.
- Report structure.
- Scope exclusions.

Success metric:

- The offer can be explained in under 30 seconds and fulfilled manually.

### Role: `sales_strategist`

Objective:

Validate willingness to pay for `$49/site/mo`.

Required deliverables:

- Prospect criteria.
- Outreach script.
- Discovery questions.
- Objection log.
- Close script.

Success metric:

- 10 paid subscriptions in 30 days.

### Role: `marketing_growth`

Objective:

Find the first distribution channels for the subscription.

Required deliverables:

- 3 community post ideas.
- 3 landing page angles.
- 1 lead magnet concept.
- Channel success metrics.

Success metric:

- At least 10 qualified calls or paid signups from non-friend traffic.

### Role: `copywriter`

Objective:

Write direct, low-hype subscription copy.

Required deliverables:

- Landing page hero.
- Offer section.
- FAQ.
- Cold email.
- Reddit/community post draft.

Success metric:

- Copy makes `$49/site/mo` feel clear, low-risk, and recurring.

### Role: `brand_strategist`

Objective:

Keep naming, positioning, and page hierarchy clear enough for paid validation.

Required deliverables:

- Product naming recommendations.
- Messaging hierarchy.
- Landing page clarity review.
- Trust and differentiation notes.

Success metric:

- Prospects understand the offer, price, and output within 30 seconds.

### Role: `legal_policy_reviewer`

Objective:

Review public policy pages and product claims for Paddle-readiness and buyer clarity. This role does not replace licensed legal counsel.

Required deliverables:

- Terms, privacy, and refund policy risk notes.
- Buyer support and cancellation clarity review.
- Data handling and claim-risk review.
- Counsel-review checklist before launch.

Success metric:

- Public pages are internally consistent, low-confusion, and ready for final legal/operator review before Paddle submission.

### Role: `customer_success`

Objective:

Make manual delivery repeatable and retention-oriented.

Required deliverables:

- Onboarding checklist.
- First-report delivery checklist.
- Feedback questions.
- Expansion prompt for adding another site.

Success metric:

- First report delivered with enough value that customer keeps subscription.

### Role: `analytics_experimenter`

Objective:

Track whether this is a real recurring business signal.

Required deliverables:

- Funnel metrics.
- Pricing feedback tracker.
- Fulfillment time tracker.
- Retention readout.

Success metric:

- Clear proceed, pivot, or kill recommendation after 30 days.

### Role: `seo_aeo_geo_expert`

Objective:

Ensure the brief is SEO-correct and avoids AI/GEO overclaiming.

Required deliverables:

- Prompt/query sampling rules.
- AI visibility language guardrails.
- Recommendation quality checklist.

Success metric:

- Customer-facing brief is useful without promising rankings or AI visibility.
