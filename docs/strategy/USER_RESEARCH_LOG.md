# USER RESEARCH LOG

Last updated: 2026-06-14

Use this to track customer discovery before treating outreach as product validation.

## Research Goal

Understand how solo SEO consultants and micro-agencies actually create monthly client updates, decide next actions, and explain search visibility.

The current product idea stays in the background. The first job is to find repeated workflow pain.

## Question Bank

Use one or two at a time.

1. How do you currently decide what goes into a monthly SEO update for a smaller client?
2. When was the last time that workflow felt annoying or slow?
3. What tools or templates do you use for those updates today?
4. What part still needs manual judgment?
5. What do clients usually ask after receiving the update?
6. What do you leave out because it takes too long?
7. What happens when Search Console data is messy or inconclusive?
8. Have you tried to automate this before? What broke?
9. Where does AI search visibility come up, if at all, in client reporting?
10. What would make the next client update easier to send?
11. Have you seen the new GSC generative AI reports in any client accounts yet, and has that changed what you include in monthly updates?
12. When a client already has a dashboard, what still needs your judgment before the update is useful?

## Evidence Fields

For every useful reply or call, capture:

| Date | Prospect | Source | Current workflow | Tools used | Frequency | Pain | Existing workaround | Quote | Follow up | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Signal values:

1. `high`: recent real workflow, named tools, repeated pain, cost or time evidence.
2. `medium`: real workflow, but pain or urgency unclear.
3. `low`: polite interest, opinion, feature idea, or vague answer.

## Pitch Rule

Do not count a reply as validation because they said DuckRadar sounds useful.

Count it only when they describe a current workflow, a repeated pain, or a workaround they already use.
