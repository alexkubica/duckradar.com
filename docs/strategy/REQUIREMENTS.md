# REQUIREMENTS

## Goal

Validate a small recurring SEO/AEO/GEO subscription before building software.

Near-term goal:

Run workflow discovery first, then pitch only after repeated pain is visible.

## Target User

Solo SEO consultants and micro-agencies managing 3-20 client sites.

First validation wedge:

Local SEO consultants and micro-agencies managing small-business or local/service-business client sites.

## Problem

They pay for or consider broad SEO tools, but only need a small recurring workflow:

- Find monthly GSC opportunities.
- See where competitors are winning.
- Provide a lightweight, sampled AI/search visibility snapshot for selected keywords or prompts.
- Use first-party GSC generative AI performance data when the property has access, while clearly labeling unavailable or low-data reports.
- Send the client a clear action plan.

For the local SEO wedge, the painful version is:

- Explain whether the client shows up in local/service AI-style recommendations.
- Identify which directories, reviews, listicles, GBP facts, or competitor pages shape those answers.
- Turn that messy visibility layer into 3 actions the agency can execute.

## Offer

Name: DuckRadar

Price: `$49/site/mo`

Billing:

- Monthly.
- Cancel anytime.
- No setup fee during validation.

## Scope

One subscribed site gets one monthly action brief.

Inputs:

- GSC read-only access.
- GSC Performance CSV export is acceptable for the manual MVP if OAuth/API access is not available.
- GA4 read-only access if available.
- GSC generative AI performance report data if available on the property.
- Domain.
- 2 competitors.
- 3 money pages.
- 10 high-intent keywords or prompts.
- Short customer/ICP note.

Deliverables:

- 1-page client-ready action brief.
- Top 5 GSC opportunities.
- 2 competitor content/page gaps.
- 10 keyword or prompt visibility checks.
- Lightweight, sampled AI/search visibility snapshot for selected keywords or prompts. Results are directional, non-exhaustive, and do not guarantee inclusion in AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, or other AI-generated answers.
- First-party GSC generative AI performance observations when available, with a clear note when unavailable or too sparse to interpret.
- 3 prioritized actions.
- 1 content refresh or new-page brief.

For local SEO validation reports, the prompt checks should be anchored to service, city, competitor, and source visibility.

## Non-Goals

- No full SEO audit.
- No backlink research.
- No daily rank tracking.
- No content writing.
- No client calls by default.
- No CMS access required.
- No Wix, WordPress, or website admin access required.
- No technical crawl beyond obvious GSC/indexing red flags.
- No promise of rankings or deterministic AI visibility.

## Constraints

- Fulfillment target: under 30 minutes/site/month after first setup.
- Validation period: 30 days.
- Build constraint: no production app until paid subscriptions exist.
- Data constraint: use GSC-first analysis and light manual/provider checks.
- Claim constraint: follow SEO claim guardrails.

## Acceptance Criteria

- 15 workflow conversations with solo SEO consultants or micro-agencies.
- 5 high signal replies or calls with current workflow details.
- 3 repeated pains across independent prospects.
- 3 prospects ask to see DuckRadar after discussing workflow.
- 10 paid subscriptions at `$49/site/mo` after research confirms repeated pain.
- 5 subscribers remain after first monthly brief.
- 3 subscribers request multi-site expansion or add another site.
- At least 5 customers say the monthly brief replaces or reduces need for another SEO tool.
- Manual fulfillment can be compressed under 30 minutes/site/month.
