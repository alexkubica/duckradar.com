# Revenue Sprint 2026-06-17

## Diagnosis

DuckRadar is not blocked by the website, checkout, sample report, or fulfillment method.

It is blocked by lack of live buyer contact and lack of paid asks.

The current outbound loop has produced sent emails, but no conversations, no workflow evidence, and no revenue. More small cold batches may still be useful, but they should not be the main validation engine this week.

Fresh cold email from the new sender domain should remain research first. Do not include price, checkout links, attachments, tracking, HTML, or feature lists in first touch cold email.

Use the direct paid ask with warm contacts, referrals, existing relationships, social conversations, form replies, and prospects who have already described a real workflow.

## Sprint Goal

Get the first paid DuckRadar customer or prove quickly that the current offer is not strong enough.

Window:

2026-06-17 to 2026-06-24.

Primary goal:

3 paid pilot attempts where a real buyer with an active workflow is asked to pay `$49` for one site.

Minimum acceptable progress:

5 live buyer conversations with consultants, micro-agency owners, or site owners who currently use GSC or need monthly SEO reporting.

## Revenue Offer

Founding client brief.

Price:

`$49` for one site for the first month through the existing DuckRadar checkout.

What they get:

One client ready monthly SEO action brief using their GSC export, domain, money pages, competitors, and a small search and AI visibility snapshot.

Delivery promise:

Within 5 business days after complete inputs.

Continuation:

If the brief is useful, keep the subscription active at `$49/site/mo`. If not, cancel before the next month.

## Positioning

Lead with the daily job:

> Turn Search Console into a monthly client update with the next SEO actions already prioritised.

Do not lead with:

- AI SEO hype.
- Dashboard language.
- A broad SEO suite comparison.
- Future automation.
- Speculative feature lists.

## Who To Ask First

Priority 1:

Warm or semi-warm people who know an SEO consultant, agency owner, marketer, or site owner with GSC access.

Priority 2:

Previously contacted prospects where the personalization was strong and they have social channels available.

Priority 3:

Best-fit prospects from the tracker who have direct evidence of monthly reporting, GSC, client updates, or local SEO retainers.

Priority 4:

Direct site owners with GSC access and enough traffic to make a monthly brief useful.

## Today's Actions

1. Send 10 warm intro requests from the user's personal channels.
2. Send 5 direct workflow DMs or contact form messages to strongest prospects with active LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, or contact forms.
3. Ask for the paid pilot only after a prospect shows a real current reporting workflow or is warm enough for a direct ask.
4. Prepare the next cold email batch only after the direct/social messages are queued.
5. Log every response in the private DuckRadar operations repository. Keep only
   the blank research template in this public repository.

## Questions To Ask In Live Conversations

Ask about facts, not opinions:

1. When did you last prepare a monthly SEO update for a client?
2. What did you open first?
3. What data made it into the client version?
4. What did you leave out because it was too messy or not worth explaining?
5. What part took the most manual judgment?
6. Did the client ask what to do next?
7. If someone gave you a clean first draft from GSC, what would still need changing before you sent it?
8. Do you have one smaller client update due this month where this would save time?

Only after a real workflow appears:

> I can do the first DuckRadar brief for one site for `$49` this month. You send the GSC export, domain, competitors, and money pages. I send back the client update within 5 business days. Want to try it on that client?

## Success Criteria

Proceed if by 2026-06-24:

- At least 1 paid customer starts.
- Or at least 3 prospects ask to see a sample, checkout, or input requirements after describing a real workflow.
- Or at least 5 live conversations reveal the same reporting pain.

## Pivot Criteria

Rethink the offer if by 2026-06-24:

- 20 direct paid pilot asks produce zero serious conversations.
- Prospects like the idea but will not risk `$49`.
- The repeated pain is reporting presentation, not SEO prioritization.
- Buyers want a one time audit but not monthly monitoring.

## Guardrails

- Do not send high volume cold email from the new domain.
- Do not keep adding research sources while avoiding buyer asks.
- Do not promise rankings, AI visibility gains, or client outcomes.
- Do not build automation before a paid workflow repeats.
- Keep every outreach specific to the recipient's actual workflow.
