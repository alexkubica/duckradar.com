# OFFER

## Plain English Offer

We sell a monthly SEO decision-brief product for consultants and micro-agencies.

The prospect is not buying an SEO tool yet. They are buying a recurring monthly answer to:

> "Which SEO/content action should I tell this client to do next?"

## Target Buyer

Solo SEO consultants and micro-agencies managing 3-20 client sites.

First validation wedge:

Local SEO consultants and micro-agencies managing small-business or local/service-business client sites.

They are the buyer because:

- They already understand SEO.
- They have multiple client sites.
- They need monthly reporting and prioritization.
- They feel Semrush/Ahrefs-style tools are expensive or bloated.
- They can resell or pass through a per-site subscription.
- For local SEO, AI/search visibility can be checked with concrete service + city + competitor prompts instead of abstract "GEO" claims.

Best-fit buyers have enough client margin to absorb or pass through `$49/site/mo`. Ultra-low-retainer operators charging roughly `$100-$150/mo` per client are useful research subjects, but may be too price-sensitive for the first paid validation batch.

## Product Name

DuckRadar

## Price

`$49/site/mo`

## What The Customer Gets

One subscribed client site gets one monthly client-ready decision brief.

Each monthly brief includes:

- Top 5 Google Search Console opportunities.
- 2 competitor or SERP gaps.
- 10 keyword/prompt visibility checks.
- Lightweight, sampled AI/search visibility snapshot for selected keywords or prompts. Results are directional, non-exhaustive, and do not guarantee inclusion in AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, or other AI-generated answers.
- If available in the customer's Search Console property, first-party Google generative AI performance observations. If the report is unavailable or has too little data, say that clearly and do not treat it as zero visibility.
- 3 prioritized actions for next month.
- 1 content refresh or new-page brief.

For the local SEO validation wedge, the 10 prompt checks should focus on service, city, competitor, and source visibility, such as:

- "best [service] in [city]"
- "who should I call for [problem] in [city]"
- "[business] vs [competitor]"
- "is [business] a good option for [service]"

## What We Need For A Real Paid Report

Best case:

- Google Search Console read-only access.
- GA4 read-only access if available.
- Search Console generative AI performance report access if it is available on the property.
- Domain.
- 2 competitors.
- 3 money pages.
- 10 keywords or prompts.

Acceptable manual MVP:

- GSC Performance CSV export instead of API/OAuth access.
- The customer exports the last 3 months of queries/pages from GSC and sends CSV files.

Not required:

- Wix access.
- WordPress access.
- CMS access.
- Ability to edit the website.

We are recommending actions, not implementing them.

## What We Can Do Without GSC

Without GSC, we can produce a public-only sample report.

Public-only report can include:

- Crawlability basics.
- Sitemap/robots checks.
- Page structure.
- Titles and meta descriptions.
- Schema checks.
- Obvious content gaps.
- Competitor/SERP observations.
- Suggested prompts for AI/search visibility tracking.

Public-only report cannot include:

- Actual queries receiving impressions.
- GSC-reported average position data.
- Click-through-rate opportunities.
- Decaying pages.
- Pages close to ranking.
- Real monthly progress.

Use public-only reports as proof samples and lead magnets, not the core paid product.

## What We Are Not Selling

- Not a full SEO audit.
- Not implementation.
- Not backlink research.
- Not daily rank tracking.
- Not content writing.
- Not a Semrush/Ahrefs clone.
- Not "rank in ChatGPT" or "GEO hacks."

## Why This Should Be Subscription-Based

SEO consultants need recurring client updates. A one-time audit does not validate recurring revenue.

The monthly subscription is valuable if the customer says:

- "This helps me decide what to do next."
- "I can send this to my client."
- "This saves me from opening five tools."
- "I want this for another client site."

## Manual Test Options

### Option 1: Public Sample

Use a public site like `authentix.co.il`.

Goal:

- Show what the report looks like.
- Use it in outreach.
- Demonstrate judgment.

Weakness:

- No real GSC opportunities.

### Option 2: GSC CSV Report

Ask a prospect to export GSC data and send CSVs.

Goal:

- Deliver the real value without building OAuth/API yet.
- Avoid needing CMS or website access.

Required exports:

- Queries report, last 3 months.
- Pages report, last 3 months.
- Queries + pages if available.

### Option 3: Paid Subscription

Ask the prospect to pay `$49/site/mo`, then send onboarding data.

Goal:

- Validate real recurring demand.

Success signal:

- They pay.
- They use the brief.
- They keep subscription after first report.
- They add another site.

## Recommended First CTA

Research-first CTA:

> How are you currently deciding what goes into monthly SEO updates for smaller clients?

Follow-up CTA after workflow pain is confirmed:

> Want me to show you the current DuckRadar version and you can tell me where it misses?

Paid CTA after interest is specific:

> Start one site for `$49/mo`

Secondary CTA:

> Send me a sample report

Local SEO validation CTA:

> Would a `$49/mo` client-ready brief that combines GSC opportunities with a small AI/local visibility snapshot be useful for one local SEO client you manage?

Avoid:

- Fake purchase buttons.
- Pure waitlists as the main CTA.
- Free audits with no payment path.
- Asking prospects to predict whether an idea would be useful before they describe their real workflow.
