# Next Validation Sprint

Date: 2026-06-14

## Goal

Keep validation moving while waiting for replies from the first cold email batch.

Do not increase send volume blindly. Use the waiting time to improve the next batch, proof asset, and reply handling.

## Hypothesis

Solo SEO consultants and micro-agencies will engage more when DuckRadar asks about their current reporting workflow than when it pitches a tool.

The strongest current trigger is:

> Search Console and AI/search visibility reporting are changing, but consultants still need to decide what goes into a useful monthly client update.

## Segment

Primary:

Solo SEO consultants and micro-agencies managing 3 to 20 client sites.

Best current subsegments:

- Local SEO consultants handling GBP, GA4, GSC, calls, rankings, and client reporting.
- Consultants who mention Looker Studio, Data Studio, GSC, GA4, or monthly SEO reports.
- Consultants commenting publicly on GSC generative AI reports or AI visibility measurement.

## Active Workstreams

### 1. Reply Handling

Create a short reply kit for common prospect responses:

- curious
- what is DuckRadar
- too busy
- already uses dashboards
- AI visibility is vanity
- asks for a sample
- asks for price
- not interested
- unsubscribe
- wants a call

Success:

Every reply can be answered in under 3 minutes without drifting into a hard pitch.

### 2. Proof Asset

Use `reports/authentix-client-update-example-2026-06-14.md` as the short proof asset.

The asset should prove:

- What changed
- Why it matters
- What to do next
- What GSC would add
- How AI/search visibility is handled conservatively

Success:

A prospect can understand the output in 60 seconds.

### 3. Next Prospect Queue

Build 10 to 15 more candidates from public sources.

Search triggers:

```text
"Search Console" "AI visibility" "client reports" "SEO consultant"
"GBP data" "GA4" "local SEO reporting" "consultant"
"Looker Studio" "Search Console" "monthly SEO report" "freelance SEO"
"AI visibility tools are" "SEO" "consultant"
"GSC generative AI" "SEO consultant"
```

Rules:

- Public business identity only.
- Website or clear professional profile required.
- Contact route required before outreach.
- Forum usernames alone are not leads.
- Competitors and experts can be tracked, but should not enter buyer outreach without a clear reason.

### 4. Discovery Script

Create a 5-question call or email script that asks about past and current behavior.

Core question:

> When a client already has a dashboard, what still needs your judgment before the update is useful?

New trigger question:

> Have you seen the new GSC generative AI reports in any client accounts yet, and has that changed what you include in monthly updates?

## Metrics

This sprint should produce:

- 1 reply-handling kit.
- 1 short proof asset.
- 10 to 15 new researched candidates.
- 3 to 5 tomorrow-ready outreach messages for approval.
- No unapproved cold emails sent.

## Kill Or Pivot Criteria

Do not expand sending if:

- Any bounce, spam, or sender reputation issue appears.
- Replies show only polite interest and no workflow detail.
- Prospects consistently say AI visibility reporting is noise and do not mention another reporting pain.

Pivot the next batch toward broader GSC monthly reporting if:

- GSC AI reporting triggers attention but not pain.
- Consultants describe dashboard fatigue more than AI uncertainty.

## Next Decision

After the next 3 to 5 approved messages:

- If replies mention real workflow pain, continue research-first outreach and offer sample on request.
- If no replies, improve prospect quality and try more social/contact-form channels.
- If replies ask for a sample, send the short Authentix client update example first, not the long audit.
